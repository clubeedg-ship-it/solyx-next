The content assistant — live since 19 August

solyx.oopuo.nl. A change to the website is asked for in plain language instead of being done by hand in WordPress.

  • Threads keep their history; titles are generated from the first message.
  • A drafts panel reads live from WordPress, with previews of every draft.
  • A page browser shows every published page as a card.

Safety, as it stands today

  • The assistant edits text only.
  • It cannot delete a page.
  • It cannot touch a page that is already published.
  • It cannot change a setting.

These are audited properties of the account it runs under, not promises in code.
